# ADR-002 — Narrow the revision/handoff research gate

Status: **NARROW — Day 3 feasibility may continue; no product or demand validation.**

Decision date: September 13, 2026. Day labels are work packages completed early under authorization; the program schedule remains September 13–October 6 with 106 planned agent-hours. No actual productive hours are inferred.

## Evidence and decision

The [research brief](../day02/research-brief.md) records eight freshly retrieved products/projects plus an authored manual baseline. The [matrix](../day02/competitor-matrix.csv) gives per-capability IDs. This is public-document research, not a product trial.

Reject broad claims of novelty in versioned care plans or renewed sign-off: Carionex advertises both (S06). Sourced education and quizzes also overlap with Saana (S09). These are vendor claims, sufficient to undermine novelty rhetoric but insufficient to validate implementation. [Carionex](https://www.carionex.co.uk/solutions/care-planning-software), [Saana](https://saana.app/).

Continue only a bounded feasibility investigation of exact source/version anchors, nonclinical task dependencies, stale acknowledgement rejection and focused re-review. Preserve historical events and distinguish evidence, coordination and rehearsal state. Initial scenario: fictional appointment wording changes and transport/office-call coordination. Existing FR-01 through FR-12 and INV-01 through INV-11 remain future obligations in [scope](../day01/scope-and-acceptance.md), not checks already passed.

The intended contribution is measurable and inspectable revision behavior, if implemented and evaluated. No first-ever claim, caregiver demand, clinical benefit, technical reliability, synchronization or expert acceptance follows from this gate.

## Alternatives and tradeoff

A broad education chatbot is rejected as insufficiently differentiated and beyond the contract. A general shared task app has strong existing substitutes. A plain-document/shared-checklist comparator remains a serious alternative, with original copies, source locations and explicit dated rereview; it must not be weakened to make THREAD win. A narrower source-review-only prototype is the fallback if actionable dependencies cannot be supported. Selective invalidation may reduce redundant review, but that is an untested hypothesis and adds mapping risk.

## Falsifiers and reopen triggers

1. Does inspected documentation or a subsequently authorized demo show the full source-change → task dependency → obsolete acknowledgement → focused rehearsal chain in Carionex, Saana or another product? If yes, withdraw the gap hypothesis and compare reproducibility, accessibility and actual user needs; pivot if no meaningful research distinction remains.
2. Can consenting adult participants using fictional tasks already manage changes as accurately and with less burden using the manual baseline? If so, narrow or stop the organizer direction. No sessions have occurred.
3. Can Day 3 preserve exact anchors and reject a stale revision event without fabricated correspondence? If not, use pasted supported text or fixed fields. If even bounded mappings fail, pivot to source inspection; do not expand model inference.
4. Does document-wide fallback invalidate so much that targeted review offers no useful advantage? If yes, drop claims of selective benefit and reassess against whole-document rereview.
5. Do participants interpret acknowledgement or rehearsal as permission to follow conflicting instructions? If yes, block affected flows and redesign before continuing. Review-only medication and unresolved conflicts are nonnegotiable.
6. Can a future revision ablation isolate fewer stale-current acknowledgements without simply blocking every task? If not, there is no demonstrated contribution. Report coverage, false alarms and failures alongside correctness.

Day 3 should produce feasibility evidence and explicit cuts, as specified in the [handoff](../day02/day03-handoff.md). User/qualified-reviewer availability, eligibility and organizer clarification remain pending and are separate gates. No external message, registration, purchase, publication or deployment is authorized by this ADR.
