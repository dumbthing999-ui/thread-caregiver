# Adult discovery interview guide — prepared, not conducted

## Purpose and recruitment status

Explore PA-01–06 from the [problem brief](problem-brief.md) without suggesting that THREAD is necessary or safe. The [master plan](../../../univabio-plan/MASTER_PLAN.md), section 6, proposes 6–8 consenting adults for later fictional-case usability evaluation. For discovery, target 6–8 consenting adults with an interest in coordinating everyday family logistics; caregiving experience is optional and does not require disclosure of any health history. Seek different device/access and handoff preferences without claiming a representative sample. This discovery target is not an additional achieved sample or a completed comparative usability study.

Recruitment target NOT ACHIEVED. No sessions or participants are recorded in this pack, and no recruitment messages were sent. Later usability counterbalancing and task timing require an implemented artifact and a separate protocol. A nurse, pharmacist or qualified health educator review is separately sought, not secured and not implied by volunteer participation.

## Consent script — read before questions

“We are exploring how adults understand written information and coordinate a fictional handoff. This is not medical care, and the materials are invented. Please confirm only that you are at least 18; do not provide your birth date or identity documents. Please do not share your own or anyone else's health history, medications, discharge papers or other personal medical details.

Participation is voluntary. You can skip a question, pause or stop without giving a reason. We are evaluating the materials and explanations, not your ability as a caregiver. With your permission I will take de-identified notes about how you interpret the fictional cases. There will be no audio/video recording by default. We will not put contact information or identifiable quotations in the repository. You can ask to remove your session notes using your participant code before the stated deletion cutoff. May we proceed, and may I take those notes?”

Before actual recruitment, the facilitator must specify a real withdrawal contact/channel, session code and deletion cutoff, confirm safe note storage, and obtain explicit verbal or written agreement. Do not proceed if adult status or consent is unclear. If recording is later proposed, request separate opt-in consent and explain purpose, access and deletion; ordinary participation consent is insufficient.

## Facilitation sequence

Start with neutral questions and the fictional plain documents. Do not show a proposed “solution” until the participant has interpreted them. Rotate S-01/S-02 order in later sessions to reduce order effects and record the actual order. S-03 can be explained as a hypothetical message in flight, not live app behavior. Avoid hints such as “notice the stale acknowledgement.” Tell participants they may find the tool unnecessary. Keep instructions identical where practical; record deviations. Do not time imagined application use or report task success statistics from discussion alone.

## Ten non-leading questions

1. Using only this fictional packet, what information would you look for before arranging transport, and where would you look?
2. How would you pass that information to another adult who is taking over the task?
3. What, if anything, would you want to know about who wrote or supplied these documents?
4. Here is a second fictional packet. How would you work out whether it changes anything you planned?
5. What would you do with these two appointment statements when neither document says it replaces the other?
6. If a message says “I understand,” what would that tell you, and what would it leave unknown?
7. In this fictional situation, transport was marked done under the earlier packet. What information would you want retained when a later packet appears?
8. Suppose an acknowledgement of the earlier packet arrives after the update. How would you want that represented, and why?
9. After a neutral explanation of the proposed source-review and rehearsal flow: what parts, if any, would fit your approach, and what would you leave out or change?
10. What would make a plain document, conversation or another approach preferable to this proposal? What would you need clarified before using either approach for this fictional task?

Neutral probes: “What led you to that interpretation?”, “Can you point to the wording?”, “What remains uncertain?” Do not correct during an observation except to stop unsafe real-world discussion; debrief misconceptions afterward without medical advice.

## Falsification and stop signals

- Plain documents are sufficient and version review adds no valued help: weaken PA-01/03, not “users need education.”
- “Acknowledged” repeatedly means medically approved or administered to participants: weaken PA-02; reconsider vocabulary and task gating.
- People treat conflicting quotations as permission to choose: weaken PA-04 and block any interpretation-based safety claim.
- Rehearsal feels judgmental or obscures the source: weaken PA-05; consider removing it rather than insisting on it.
- Value depends on medication administration/treatment features: weaken PA-06; preserve non-goals rather than expand unsafely.
- A participant begins sharing health history, feels pressured or seeks advice: stop, redirect to fiction or end; do not record the health details or give treatment guidance.

Discovery offers qualitative counterexamples, not prevalence estimates, significance or clinical outcomes. Report actual participation/refusal counts only after sessions exist.

## Privacy handling for future sessions

Collect no health records, exact age, address, diagnosis or family identities. Keep recruitment contacts outside the repository in access-restricted storage separate from coded notes; contact handling remains a human responsibility, not authorized tool work today. Facilitator/research lead only by default; explicit approval is needed before wider access. Do not send raw notes to a model service or publish identifiable verbatim statements.

Proposed policy to agree before recruitment: delete contacts once scheduling/follow-up is complete; delete raw coded notes within 30 days of each session, honoring earlier withdrawal requests before aggregation; retain only consented, de-identified aggregate findings and non-identifying method notes. Tell participants the exact calendar cutoff before consent. If accidental health details are captured, remove them promptly and record only that redaction occurred. No actual data retention/deletion operation is claimed today.

Health-context framing is inherited from the plan's references to [MedlinePlus teach-back](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000460.htm) and [discharge planning](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000867.htm); neither is evidence that this interview or product has clinical approval. Do not copy their text into fictional materials.
